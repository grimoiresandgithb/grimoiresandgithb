Somewhere Beyond The Sea

Fantasy

★★★★☆

Summary

Somewhere Beyond the Sea is TJ Klune's return to the world of The House in the Cerulean Sea, , following the same blend of queer joy, found family, and soft magic that made the first bok so beloved. This sequel leans even harder into themes of healing, community, and the radical act of choosing kindness in a world that often feels too big and too cruel. It's a warm hand on your shoulder, a reminder that hope is still possible, and that chosen family can be the most powerful magic of all.

Thoughts

TJ Klune has my heart ya'll.

In this big fuck of a world, it's healing to read a story filled with queer joy and acceptance and chosen family. There's something so grounding about a book that isn't pretending to be life-changing literature, but still manages to thaw whatever frozen part of your heart you've been ignoring. Klune writes comfort like it's a spell; gentle, intentional, and quietly powerful.

This book feels like being wrapped in a blanket by someone who actually gets you. It's soft and earnest and more than a little silly, but in the way where you can feel the sincerity underneath each line. Klune understands the ache of wanting to belong, the exhaustion of surviving, and the tiny rebellions that make queer joy feel like magic.

It's not perfect, but it's not trying to be. It's trying to be kind. And honestly? That's better.
